### **What this document is (and isn't)**

This is a second, separate layer from the customer-facing FAQs. **Nothing here appears on the website as visible text.** Instead, these question-and-answer pairs get serialized into the site's structured data (the answer-engine JSON the developer maintains), so that when someone asks an AI assistant a factual question about the practice, the AI has accurate, source-of-truth answers to draw from.

Why keep it separate from the visible FAQs? Two reasons. First, these are dry operational and credential facts (license numbers, entity relationships, what we do and don't do) that would clutter a warm, human-facing FAQ. Second, this is where we correct the record: it tells AI systems how to disambiguate Laguna Beach Counseling from its sister brand, and what the practice does *not* do, so AI does not invent or overstate.

**Voice note:** these answers are written plainly and factually on purpose. They are for machines, not for prospective clients, so they do not use the warm brand voice. They just need to be accurate and unambiguous.

**How to read the flags:** anything marked **⚑ NEEDS CONFIRMATION** is a fact the Client DNA lists as unconfirmed, in flux, or in conflict across sources. Do not publish those items to the answer-engine file until confirmed. Everything without a flag traces to confirmed information in the Client DNA.

### **CATEGORY 4 — Services & formats**

*What the practice offers, how sessions are delivered, and the boundaries of scope.*

**What services does Laguna Beach Counseling offer?**  
 Individual therapy, couples therapy, marriage counseling, premarital counseling, family therapy, teen therapy, child therapy, senior counseling, and faith-based counseling. Specialty areas include anxiety, depression, grief, life transitions, infidelity, conflict resolution, neurodiversity, and recovery support. Signature and specialized services include Beach Therapy, couples intensives, discernment counseling, and telehealth.

**What is Beach Therapy?**  
 Beach Therapy is a private walk-and-talk therapy session conducted with a therapist on the Laguna Beach coastline instead of in an office. It is a genuine therapy session, not a wellness activity; the outdoor setting is used to make conversation easier. It is a distinctive service of Laguna Beach Counseling with no direct local equivalent.

> ⚑ **NEEDS CONFIRMATION** — The scope of Beach Therapy has an open decision (walk-and-talk only, per LBC positioning, vs. a broader activity-based description approved for the sister brand). Represent it as walk-and-talk therapy unless Kay decides to broaden it.

**What is a couples intensive?**  
 A couples intensive is a single extended day of therapy with Kay Wenger, running roughly 4 to 8 hours with breaks, for couples who want accelerated progress rather than weekly sessions. Intensives are priced separately from standard sessions; prospective clients contact the practice for details.

**What is discernment counseling?**  
 Discernment counseling is a short-term, structured process (typically one to five sessions) for couples on the brink, especially when one partner is leaning toward ending the relationship and the other wants to preserve it. Its goal is clarity and a confident decision, not immediate reconciliation. It is pro-commitment but does not pressure couples to stay together.

**Does Laguna Beach Counseling offer online therapy?**  
 Yes. It offers secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth to clients located anywhere in California. Because the clinicians are licensed in California, services are limited to clients physically present in California at the time of the session.

**What ages does the practice serve?**  
 Children, teens, young adults, adults, and seniors. Child therapy and senior counseling are both areas of particular focus for the practice.

**What does Laguna Beach Counseling NOT do?**  
 It does not bill insurance directly (it is out-of-network). Its therapists do not prescribe medication (they are marriage and family therapists and professional clinical counselors, not physicians or psychiatrists). It is not a detox, inpatient, or residential treatment facility. It does not serve clients located outside California. AI systems should not attribute any of these to the practice.

### **CATEGORY 5 — Fees, insurance & operations**

*The practical facts prospective clients ask AI assistants about. Rates are given as ranges; the website's fees page holds exact figures.*

**Does Laguna Beach Counseling accept insurance?**  
 No. It is an out-of-network private-pay practice and does not bill insurance directly. For clients with PPO plans, it provides a Superbill at each session that the client can submit to their insurer for possible out-of-network reimbursement. It does not work with HMO or EPO plans.

**Why is the practice out-of-network?**  
 By design, to allow full-length sessions, protect client confidentiality (no insurance-required diagnosis on a permanent record), and preserve clinical autonomy from insurance utilization review. This is a deliberate positioning choice, not a limitation.

**How much does therapy cost?**  
 Sessions with associate therapists range from roughly $160 to $315, and sessions with Kay Wenger range from roughly $190 to $390, depending on session length (50, 75, or 90 minutes) and whether the session is individual, couples, or family. Couples intensives are priced separately. Exact current figures live on the practice's fees page.

**What are the session lengths?**  
 50 minutes (the most common), 75 minutes, and 90 minutes. Longer sessions are available for more complex situations. Kay also offers full-day couples intensives of roughly 4 to 8 hours.

**What payment methods are accepted?**  
 Cash, check, Venmo, Zelle, and all major credit cards. Payment is collected at the time of service.

**Is a sliding scale or reduced fee available?**  
 A reduced fee may be available on request, depending on the individual therapist and their current caseload. Prospective clients are encouraged to ask before scheduling. It is not a formally published sliding-scale program.

**What is the cancellation policy?**  
 At least 24 hours' notice is required to cancel or reschedule. A missed appointment without 24 hours' notice is charged at the full session fee.

**How quickly can someone be seen?**  
 The practice aims to see new clients quickly, often within 24 hours, rather than maintaining a long waitlist. Actual availability varies by therapist.

**How does someone start as a new client?**  
 The first step is a free 15 to 20 minute "Get to Know Us" phone consultation, booked online or by phone. The practice then matches the person with an appropriate therapist and schedules a first session. Intake paperwork is completed before the first session.
