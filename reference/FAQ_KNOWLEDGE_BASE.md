# **/about/our-approach**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *our therapy approach*

**What is your approach to therapy?**

We believe relationships heal people, not techniques. Tools and methods matter, but they only work when you feel understood by the person across from you. So we slow down, get to know what is really going on, and then help you move.

That means you leave sessions with two things: the sense that you were heard, and something practical to work with. [Book a free fit call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started) to see how it feels.

**How long will I need to be in therapy?**

It depends on what you are working on, and we will be honest with you about it. Some people come for a specific issue and finish in a few months. Others value ongoing support. We work toward your goals, not toward keeping you in the room.

**Will therapy actually change anything?**

It can, and the changes are usually pretty ordinary in the best way. Conversations that used to end in arguments get easier. Decisions get clearer. You spend less energy managing anxiety or resentment and have more left for actually living.

The goal is not a perfect life. It is having the tools and resilience to handle a real one. [Start with a free call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

# **/therapy/individuals  ·  GROWTH PRIORITY**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *individual therapy Laguna Beach*

**What can individual therapy help with?**

Individual therapy helps with anxiety, depression, stress and burnout, self-esteem, grief, life transitions, and the quieter sense that something needs to change even when you cannot name it. You do not need to be in crisis to benefit.

If a specific concern brought you here, read about [anxiety therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/specialties/anxiety), [depression therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/specialties/depression), or [counseling for life transitions](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/specialties/life-transitions).

**How do I know if I need therapy or if I am just stressed?**

A good rule of thumb: if something has been weighing on you long enough that you are wondering whether this is just how it is going to feel, that is reason enough to talk to someone. You do not have to hit a crisis to deserve support.

**What if I have tried therapy before and it did not help?**

That is worth talking about, not a reason to give up. When someone tells us therapy did not help before, we do not assume they were resistant. Usually it just was not the right fit, and fit is exactly what we pay attention to.

The [free Get to Know Us call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started) is a low-pressure way to find out whether one of our therapists is the right person this time.

# **/therapy/couples  ·  premarital merges in here**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *couples therapy Laguna Beach*

**When should a couple start therapy?**

Sooner than most couples think. You do not have to be in crisis. If you are having the same argument over and over, feeling more like roommates than partners, or sensing a distance you cannot explain, that is a good time to come in.

If trust has been broken, see [infidelity and affair recovery](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/specialties/infidelity). If you are unsure whether to stay together at all, [discernment counseling](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/discernment-counseling) may be the right first step.

**Do you offer premarital counseling?**

Yes, and it is some of our favorite work, because it helps couples build strong habits before the hard seasons arrive rather than after. It covers communication, expectations, money, family, and the conversations that are easy to skip while planning a wedding.

Premarital work is part of our [couples therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/couples). Start with a [free consultation](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

**Do we always meet together, or can we each talk to the therapist alone?**

Most couples sessions are held with both of you together, which is where the real work happens. That said, there is room for each of you to meet with the therapist individually when it helps, so both partners feel heard.

Sessions run 50 to 90 minutes, in person or [online anywhere in California](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/telehealth).

**Will a therapist take sides, or push us to stay together or split up?**

Neither. We are pro-commitment but not attached to a particular outcome. The job is not to push you toward staying or leaving, it is to help you both get clear, talk honestly, and make a decision you can actually live with.

When a couple is genuinely on the fence, [discernment counseling](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/discernment-counseling) is a short, structured way to find clarity first.

# **/therapy/families**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *family therapy Laguna Beach*

**What does family therapy help with?**

Family therapy helps when the tension is between people rather than inside one person: parent and teen conflict, communication that keeps breaking down, big transitions, or the long work of rebuilding trust. We help the family work as a system rather than looking for someone to blame.

If the concern centers on a teenager or younger child, you may also want [teen therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/teens) or [child therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/children).

**Does the whole family have to come to every session?**

Not necessarily. Family therapy is flexible. Sometimes everyone meets together, sometimes the therapist works with a pair or an individual for a session, depending on what will help most. Your therapist will shape it around your family.

**Can family therapy help with a teenager who will not open up?**

Often, yes. A skilled therapist gives a reluctant teen room to trust the process at their own pace, and helps the rest of the family communicate in a way that invites the teen in rather than pushing them further out.

Learn more about [therapy for teens](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/teens), or [book a free call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

# **/therapy/teens  ·  mother-and-daughter section lives here**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *teen therapist Laguna Beach*

**How do I know if my teenager needs therapy?**

Watch for changes that last more than a couple of weeks: pulling away from friends or family, slipping grades, sleeping or eating very differently, ongoing irritability or sadness, or losing interest in things they used to love. When those stick around, therapy can help.

We work with teens on anxiety, depression, anger, and self-esteem, and this is also home to [mother-and-daughter counseling](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/teens).

**Will you tell me what my teen says in session?**

Not word for word, and that is what makes the therapy work. Teens need to trust that they can speak freely. Your therapist keeps you involved in the big picture and will always tell you about genuine safety concerns, while protecting the day-to-day confidentiality your teen needs.

**Do you offer therapy for young adults?**

Yes. The stretch from the late teens into the twenties has its own challenges, from identity and independence to career pressure and relationships. We help young adults navigate that season with support that fits where they actually are.

This overlaps with our [individual therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/individuals). Start with a [free consultation](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

**How does therapy work for a young child?**

For children, therapy often looks like play, art, and activity rather than sitting and talking, because that is how kids naturally express what they feel. The therapist builds trust first, then helps the child work through what is going on in a way that fits their age.

We help children with anxiety, big feelings, family changes, grief, and school stress, and we keep parents involved throughout. [Book a free consultation](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

**How do I know if my child needs to see a therapist?**

Trust your instincts, and watch for changes that last: new fears or clinginess, trouble sleeping, big shifts in mood or behavior, withdrawal, or struggles at school. If something feels off for more than a few weeks, a conversation with a therapist can help.

**Will I be involved in my child's therapy?**

Yes. With younger children especially, parents are part of the work. Your therapist keeps you informed, gives you practical ways to support your child at home, and partners with you rather than working around you.

# **/specialties/anxiety  ·  GROWTH PRIORITY**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *anxiety therapist Laguna Beach*

**How do I know if I have anxiety or if I am just a worrier?**

A useful line: worry visits, anxiety moves in. If the worry is constant, hard to switch off, and starting to affect your sleep, your body, your work, or your relationships, that is worth talking to someone about. You do not have to wait until it is unbearable.

Anxiety convinces you the worst-case scenario is also the most likely one. That is not a character flaw, it is your nervous system doing its job a little too well. Understanding that is often where the work starts.

We help with generalized anxiety, panic attacks, phobias, and social anxiety. [Book a free call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

**Can therapy actually help with panic attacks?**

Yes. Panic attacks feel dangerous but are treatable, and therapy is one of the most effective ways to reduce how often they happen and how much they frighten you. You can learn what is happening in your body and how to respond to it differently.

**Does anxiety therapy help with social anxiety and phobias?**

Yes. Social anxiety and specific phobias both respond well to therapy. The work helps you understand what is driving the fear and build real, gradual confidence in the situations that have felt off-limits, at a pace that feels manageable.

This is part of our [individual therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/individuals) for adults, teens, and children.

# **/specialties/depression  ·  GROWTH PRIORITY**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *depression therapist Laguna Beach*

**How do I know if it is depression or just a rough patch?**

The clue is usually time and reach. A rough patch lifts. Depression tends to stay for weeks and spread into everything, draining your energy, your interest, and your hope. If getting through an ordinary day has started to feel like a lot, it is worth talking to someone.

Depression is treatable, and you do not have to wait until you hit bottom. [Start with a free consultation](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

*If you are ever in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please call or text 988 (the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or go to your nearest emergency room. Therapy is important, but it is not emergency care.*

**Can therapy help depression without medication?**

For many people, yes. Therapy on its own helps a great deal with depression, and some people prefer to start there. For others, therapy works alongside medication prescribed by a physician. We help you think through what fits you, and coordinate with your doctor when that is useful.

**I do not even have the energy to start. What is the first step?**

Start as small as it needs to be. One free 15 to 20 minute phone call, where you do not have to explain everything or have it figured out. Depression makes starting feel enormous, so we keep that very first step as light as possible.

When you are ready, [book the free call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started), or call (949) 357-3587.

# **/specialties/grief  ·  links to seniors \+ Susi Q**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *grief counseling Laguna Beach*

**How long does grief last, and when should I get help?**

There is no timeline for grief, and anyone who gives you one is wrong. Grief is not a problem to solve, it is something to carry, and it changes shape over time. Getting help makes sense whenever the weight of it is more than you can hold alone.

We support people through every kind of loss. Grief work connects closely with our [senior counseling](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/seniors). Reach out for a [free call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started) when you are ready.

**Is it normal to still be grieving after a long time?**

Completely normal. Grief does not run on a schedule, and it can resurface around anniversaries, holidays, or out of nowhere. That does not mean something is wrong with you. It means the loss mattered. Therapy can help you carry it.

*If you are ever in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please call or text 988 (the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or go to your nearest emergency room. Therapy is important, but it is not emergency care.*

**Can you help with grief that is not about a death?**

Yes. Grief follows many kinds of loss: a divorce, a diagnosis, a move, a job, a friendship, the life you thought you would have. All of it is real, and all of it is worth support. You do not have to justify your grief to work on it.

This overlaps with our [life transitions counseling](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/specialties/life-transitions).

# **/specialties/life-transitions**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *life transition counseling*

**Can therapy help with a major life change even if nothing is wrong?**

Yes. You do not need a problem to benefit from support through a transition. New job, new city, new baby, empty nest, divorce, retirement, a diagnosis. Even good changes ask a lot of us, and having someone to think it through with helps you land on your feet.

This is core to our [individual therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/individuals). [Book a free consultation](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

**Is it normal to feel lost even when the change is a good one?**

Very. A promotion, a wedding, a move somewhere beautiful, a long-awaited retirement. Good changes still ask you to let go of something familiar, and that can leave you unexpectedly unmoored. That feeling is worth taking seriously, not brushing off.

**How is transition counseling different from regular therapy?**

It is regular therapy, focused. Instead of open-ended work, it centers on the specific change you are moving through: what you are losing, what you are stepping into, and how to steady yourself in between. Many people come for a defined stretch rather than indefinitely.

# **/specialties/infidelity  ·  top presenting issue**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *infidelity counseling Laguna Beach*

**Can a relationship actually recover after infidelity?**

Yes, many do. It is hard, and it takes honesty and time from both people, but affairs do not automatically mean the end of a relationship. With the right support, a lot of couples rebuild trust, and some describe their relationship afterward as more honest than it was before.

This work is part of our [couples therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/couples), and for couples who want to move faster, a [couples intensive with Kay](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/couples-intensive) can help.

**Should we start couples therapy right away after an affair?**

When you are both willing to be in the room, yes, sooner tends to help. Early support can keep the first raw weeks from doing more damage and give you a structured, steadier place to have the hardest conversations. If one of you is unsure the relationship should continue, that is worth naming too.

If one partner is genuinely undecided, [discernment counseling](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/discernment-counseling) is designed for exactly that.

**Do you take sides if there has been an affair?**

No. We do not assign a villain and a victim. Both people are hurting, in different ways, and both need to be understood for the relationship to have a real chance. We stay curious about what happened rather than putting anyone on trial.

Start with a [free consultation](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

# **/specialties/conflict-resolution  ·  links to couples**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *conflict resolution counseling*

**We keep having the same fight. Can therapy fix that?**

Usually, yes, because the repeating fight is rarely about what it looks like on the surface. Therapy helps you see the pattern underneath the argument, the one you cannot spot while you are in it, and change how the two of you get stuck.

This is core to our [couples therapy in Laguna Beach](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/couples). [Book a free fit call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

**Is it too late if we have been fighting like this for years?**

It is rarely too late. Long-standing patterns are more worn-in, not permanent. Couples who have been stuck for years regularly find that once they can finally see the cycle clearly, it starts to loosen. The length of the pattern matters less than the willingness to look at it.

**What if only one of us wants to work on it?**

That happens often, and it is workable. It helps enormously if both partners come, but change can start even when one person is more hopeful than the other. What matters most is a willingness to show up and stay curious about the relationship as a whole.

Start with a [free call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started) and we will talk it through.

# **/beach-therapy  ·  category of one, highest link priority**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *beach therapy Laguna Beach*

**What is Beach Therapy and how is it different from regular therapy?**

Beach Therapy is a real therapy session that happens while you walk the Laguna coastline with your therapist, instead of sitting in an office. It is the same confidential, professional care, but the setting itself helps: walking side by side often makes it easier to say the hard thing.

The beach is not replacing therapy. It is creating conditions that let therapy happen differently. Sometimes the environment matters.

**What actually happens during a Beach Therapy session?**

You walk a quiet stretch of coastline with your therapist and talk, the same way you would in an office, just side by side and in motion. It is real therapy, not a beach activity: the conversation is the work, and the setting helps the words come more easily.

If a walk is not right for a given day, you can always move the session indoors or [online](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/telehealth).

**What do I wear, and what if the weather is bad?**

# **/discernment-counseling  ·  links to couples**

**TARGET KEYWORD**  *discernment counseling Laguna Beach*

**What is discernment counseling, and how is it different from couples therapy?**

Discernment counseling is short-term help for couples on the brink, especially when one person is leaning out of the relationship and the other is leaning in. It is not about fixing the marriage in the room. It is about gaining enough clarity to choose a direction with confidence, usually in one to five sessions.

It is pro-commitment, never pro-pressure. The goal is a clear-eyed decision you can live with, whichever way it goes. If you both decide to work on the relationship, that is when couples therapy begins.

**Is discernment counseling just a step toward divorce?**

No. It is a step toward clarity, and clarity sometimes leads to recommitting to the relationship with new energy. The point is to help you decide on purpose rather than by drift or exhaustion, whatever that decision turns out to be.

If you choose to work on the relationship, our [couples therapy](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/therapy/couples) is the next step.

**What if my partner and I want different things?**

That is exactly who discernment counseling is for. It is built for the situation where one of you is leaning out and the other is leaning in. Each of you gets heard, and the structure helps you reach a decision together instead of staying stuck.

Start with a [free consultation](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).

**Do you take insurance, or are you out-of-network?**

We are out-of-network and do not bill insurance directly. If you have a PPO plan, we give you a Superbill at every session that you can submit to your insurer for possible out-of-network reimbursement. We do not accept HMO or EPO plans.

We accept payment at the time of service. Whether your plan reimburses you, and how much, is between you and your insurance company, so it is worth calling them before you start.

**Why are you out-of-network instead of taking insurance?**

Because it lets us do better work. Staying out-of-network means we can offer full-length sessions, protect your privacy, and make clinical decisions based on what you need rather than what an insurance reviewer will approve.

Three real reasons sit behind it. Session length: insurance often pushes therapists toward 45-minute sessions, and we would rather give you the full 50, 75, or 90 minutes. Confidentiality: billing insurance requires putting a diagnosis on your permanent record, and out-of-network work means you can do personal or relationship work without a label following you. Autonomy: we design your care with you, not around a utilization reviewer.

**What is a Superbill, and what is your cancellation policy?**

A Superbill is an itemized receipt with everything your PPO insurer needs to consider reimbursing you: you pay at the time of service, we provide the Superbill, and you submit it. Our cancellation policy asks for at least 24 hours' notice, or the full session fee is charged.

Good questions to ask your insurer before starting:

* Do I have out-of-network mental health benefits?

* What is my deductible, and has it been met?

* How many sessions per year does my plan cover, and at what reimbursement rate?

* Is a referral from my primary care physician required?

Payment methods: cash, check, Venmo, Zelle, and all major credit cards. A reduced fee may be available on request, so ask before you schedule. [Book a free call](https://www.lagunabeachcounseling.com/get-started).
